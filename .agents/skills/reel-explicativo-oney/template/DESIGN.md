# Design — the creator's explainer reel (reel-explicativo)

## Style Prompt
Explainer / news reel about a hidden mechanism ("quem manda de verdade", "pra onde vai o dinheiro").
Sober and credible: the presenter carries the story, real footage proves, one flat diagram explains,
and the edit never competes with the argument. Support footage sits in a 16:9 band on top with a small
context tag; captions are short phrases on an opaque dark box in the same spot all video long; the
mechanism is drawn on ONE white plane whose elements change in place, step by step.

## Colors
- `#0f1a24` — caption box (100 % opaque) and context tags (`#111`)
- `#ffffff` — caption text
- `#fbfbfa` — diagram plane (near white)
- `#1c2733` — diagram text, arrows
- `#1f7a63` — the ONE accent (the actor who wins / the money / the new relation). Swap per story if
  the brand color of the protagonist says it better — still only one accent.
- `#5d6670` — secondary labels, caveats ("Acordo sujeito ao Cade.")

## Typography
- Inter only. Captions SemiBold 46 px, 1–2 lines. Diagram nodes SemiBold 88 px tight tracking,
  relation labels Medium 34 px, step label Medium 38 px caps tracked .2em, subs Regular 32 px.

## Layout (1080 × 1920)
- Support band: y 0–668 (16:9 + margin). Presenter below it. Tag top-left (56, 52). Credit bottom-right.
- Captions: box top at ~1545 px (lower third, above the IG UI), centred, max 900 px wide.
- Diagram: step label y 118, caveat y 180, content between y 450 and 1300, footer free for captions.

## What NOT to Do
- No word-by-word karaoke captions, no emojis, no Advercase, no yellow highlights.
- No zooms, slides, whooshes or camera moves inside a diagram; no boxes around nodes (text is the node).
- No AI image presented as a real place, document, logo screen or app interface. AI B-roll is generic
  and tagged `ILUSTRAÇÃO`. Archive material is tagged with its year.
- No new diagram per step: one plane per mechanism, changing in place.
